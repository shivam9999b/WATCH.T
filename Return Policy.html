Return & Refund Policy

At watch.T, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. Please read this policy before placing an order.

Important: Please contact our support team before sending any product back. Returns and refunds are subject to the eligibility conditions below.

1. Return Eligibility

If a return or replacement is permitted for your order, please contact us within the return window communicated on the product page or at the time of purchase.

  • The watch should be unused and in its original condition.
  • Original packaging, tags and included accessories should be retained.
  • Proof of purchase or your order number may be required.
  • Products damaged through misuse or accidental damage may not qualify.

2. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact us as soon as possible. Please share your order number and clear photographs or a video showing the issue. We will review the request and explain the available resolution.

3. Replacement Policy

A replacement may be offered for an eligible damaged, defective or incorrect product, subject to verification and stock availability. If a replacement is unavailable, we will explain the alternatives available for your order.

4. Refund Policy

Refunds are processed only after an eligible return or refund request has been reviewed and approved. The refund method and expected processing time will be communicated to you. Bank or payment-provider processing may take additional time.

5. Order Cancellation

If you want to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible. Cancellation may not be possible after the order has been dispatched. Any eligible refund will be handled according to the applicable payment and refund process.

6. Return Shipping

Contact our support team for return instructions before shipping any item. We will inform you about the applicable shipping arrangements and any charges, if relevant.

7. How to Request a Return

  1. Keep your order number ready.
  2. Contact watch.T support and explain the issue.
  3. Provide any requested photos or supporting information.
  4. Wait for return instructions and approval before sending the item.

8. Policy Updates

WATCH.T may update this policy when necessary. The applicable terms and any rights provided by law will continue to apply to your purchase.

Contact Us

For return, replacement or refund assistance, contact our support team using the official contact details listed on the watch.T website.

Please include your order number so we can help you more quickly.
